## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end technical luxury and precision engineering. Drawing direct cues from high-performance developer ecosystems, architectural industrial design, and professional creative hardware, the aesthetic treats digital interfaces as precision-machined tools.

### Brand Personality & Core Principles
- **Surgical Precision:** Every border, line-height, and padding increment is mathematically aligned and disciplined. Unnecessary embellishments, loud decorative gradients, or gratuitous illustrations are strictly omitted.
- **Instrument-Grade Luxury:** Surfaces emulate dark, matte physical materials—anodized titanium, deep zinc, and pitch obsidian. Contrast is deliberate: black is absolute, white is pure and luminous, and intermediary tones behave like light striking bead-blasted metal.
- **Focus & Restraint:** Color is never decorative; it is strictly functional and informative. Micro-interactions are brief, immediate, and free from bouncy physics.

### Design Movement
The visual language operates at the intersection of **Extreme Minimalism** and **Technical Utilitarianism**. Surfaces rely on micro-borders, stacked neutral luminescence, crisp geometric typography, and surgical micro-radius corners to project speed, durability, and computational power.

## Colors

The palette is strictly calibrated around deep carbon neutrality, cold zinc steps, and pure photonic white.

### Surface Architecture
- **Obsidian Ground (`#09090b`):** The foundational canvas layer. Absorbs light completely, establishing limitless depth.
- **Titanium Base (`#121214`):** Secondary container surface for elevated views, sidebars, and structural app shells.
- **Zinc Elevated (`#18181b`):** Primary card, panel, and floating surface tier.
- **Zinc Hover / Active (`#27272a`):** Interactive surface highlight, segmented toggle active states, and raised badges.

### Monochrome & Accent Levels
- **Pure White (`#ffffff`):** Reserved exclusively for primary typography, vital interactive focal points, and maximum-emphasis action triggers.
- **Off-White / Bright Titanium (`#f4f4f5`):** High-priority values, standard primary actions, and primary iconography.
- **Zinc Medium (`#a1a1aa`):** Secondary body text, active state borders, and neutral badge text.
- **Titanium Muted (`#71717a`):** Structural micro-borders, helper labels, disabled states, and inactive tab indicators.
- **Zinc Deep (`#3f3f46`):** Subdued borders, keyboard shortcut boundaries, and muted dividers.

### Surgical Semantic Overlays
Color enters the UI solely to communicate machine status:
- **Critical Ruby (`#f43f5e`, muted base `#4c0519`):** Destructive actions, system anomalies, test failures, and critical churn.
- **Attention Amber (`#f59e0b`, muted base `#451a03`):** Pipeline warnings, rate-limiting warnings, and pending review states.
- **Operational Emerald (`#10b981`, muted base `#022c22`):** Healthy nodes, deployment complete, and operational status.

## Typography

Typography functions as the structural skeleton of the product. The primary type is **Geist**, providing low geometric distortion, crisp terminal endings, and tight tracking across digital screens. 

### Typographic Roles
- **Headlines & Structural Titles:** Set with negative letter-spacing (`-0.015em` to `-0.04em`) and medium/semibold weights to evoke precision industrial branding. Headlines never use heavy bolding or decorative stylings.
- **Body & Prose:** Optimized for dense computational readability. Text utilizes neutral rendering smoothing (`-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased`) to prevent weight blooming on dark screens.
- **Labels, Telemetry, and Metas:** Handled exclusively by **JetBrains Mono**. Counters, hashes, port numbers, time stamps, and keybindings rely on monospace tabular alignment to ensure absolute dimensional consistency during data refreshes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a modular 4px/8px micro-grid engineered for density, alignment, and information architecture.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (Viewport < 768px):** Single-column stack with dynamic full-width cards, edge margins of `1rem` (`16px`), and gutters of `1rem`. Secondary telemetry shifts off-canvas into modal drawers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid structure with page margins of `1.5rem` (`24px`). Toolbars and side utility menus switch to compact iconic states.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fixed-max hybrid system (maximum application width of `1440px`), with page margins of `2.5rem` (`40px`) and `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters. Sidebars maintain fixed 240px or 280px widths while primary dashboard canvases scale fluidly.

### Density & Spacing Philosophy
Spacing tokens are tight and surgical. Interface density favors compact interaction zones over excessive whitespace. Elements are held in close contextual relationships to allow engineers and power users to parse dense real-time data sets without excessive scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation is achieved strictly through **Tonal Layering** and **Low-Contrast Micro-Borders**, rejecting traditional diffuse drop shadows.

### Border Physics (Micro-Linework)
- Ground and container depth is separated using hairline 1px borders colored in translucent zinc: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or solid `#27272a`.
- Active focus elements and elevated popovers introduce a dual-stroke illusion using a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` alongside a tight inner inset line (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).

### Atmospheric Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Obsidian - `#09090b`):** Root viewport canvas and full-bleed document backgrounds.
- **Tier 1 (Titanium Inset - `#121214`):** Fixed utility navigation, collapsibles, and secondary panels.
- **Tier 2 (Zinc Panel - `#18181b`):** Data tables, cards, toolbars, and input containers.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Overlays & Menus - `#18181b` with backdrop-filter):** Context menus, tooltips, and modal surfaces. Rendered with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle ambient occlusion of `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`. Shadows are pitch-black, short, and purely functional for edge separation.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft / Architectural** shape profile (`roundedness: 1`). Corners are disciplined, modern, and avoid both severe 90-degree brutality and playful organic pills.

### Radius Metrics
- **Micro Radius (`4px` / `0.25rem`):** Applied to dense inline components—checkboxes, keycaps, inner segments of segmented controls, tooltips, and code blocks.
- **Component Radius (`6px` to `8px` / `0.375rem` to `0.5rem`):** The primary radius applied to buttons, input fields, dropdown trigger buttons, and badges.
- **Container Radius (`8px` to `12px` / `0.5rem` to `0.75rem`):** Outer borders of cards, modals, preview viewports, and table framing containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Pure white fill (`#ffffff`), text in `#09090b`, font weight semibold. On hover, subtle step down to `#f4f4f5` with an ultra-fine white luminescence (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
- **Secondary:** Surface fill of `#18181b`, micro-border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text in `#f4f4f5`. On hover, background shifts to `#27272a` and border lightens to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, text in `#a1a1aa`. On hover, background shifts to `#18181b` and text to `#ffffff`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#121214`, border 1px solid `#27272a`, text `#f4f4f5`, placeholder `#71717a`. Focus state shifts border to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` with an inner key-light stroke, avoiding colored focus rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hairline zinc framing (`#3f3f46`) over obsidian base. When checked, the fill is `#ffffff` with a pitch-black `#09090b` checkmark.

### Cards & Panels
- **Structure:** Background `#121214` or `#18181b`, enclosed by a 1px solid border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Headers and footers are separated by 1px horizontal dividers without elevation changes.
- **Interactive Cards:** Hovering elevates the surface by lightening the border to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` and applying an extremely subtle top hairline highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Metadata Badges:** Monospace type (`JetBrains Mono`, `11px`), background `#18181b`, border 1px solid `#27272a`, text `#a1a1aa`.
- **Semantic Indicators:** Accompanied by a 6px circular indicator with an ambient glow (e.g., emerald `#10b981` dot with `box-shadow: 0 0 6px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`).

### Data Lists & Key-Value Tables
- Alternating rows avoid striped zebra fills; rows are instead separated by 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` borders. Row hover applies an instant `#18181b` wash with no transition delay.
- Keys are rendered in `#71717a` body text; values are rendered in `#ffffff` or monospace `#a1a1aa`.

### Command Palette & Keybinding Elements (KBD)
- **KBD Chips:** Inset-styled elements with background `#18181b`, border 1px solid `#3f3f46`, text `#a1a1aa`, rendered in `JetBrains Mono` at `10px` with 2px padding.
- **Command Palette:** Centered floating obsidian modal (`#09090b`), surrounded by a crisp `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` border and a deep backdrop blur scrim (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`).
